# Kings Equestrian — My Rides Portal Guide  
## For parents & riders

**Who this is for:** Parents and riders who book classes, track progress, shop for kit, and follow order status  
**What this portal does:** View sessions, book classes, see curriculum progress, order riding essentials, track payments — on phone or computer  
**No password:** Sign in with your registered **phone number** or **KE Number** only

---

## 1. Getting started

### 1.1 Open My Rides

1. Use the **My Rides link** your school / campus shared (it ends with `?app=portal`).
2. Enter your **registered phone** or **KE Number** (example: `KE240101…`).
3. Tap **View My Rides**.

**Same phone, more than one rider?**  
You will see a **Choose a Profile** screen. Pick the correct family member.

**Tip:** The portal remembers your last KE Number on this device. Tap **Sign out** when finished if you share the phone.

### 1.2 Tabs (left to right)

| Tab | Use it for |
|-----|------------|
| **Sessions** | Upcoming and past classes; reschedule if needed |
| **Curriculum** | Level / class progress and performance |
| **Book** | Book new single, multi, or recurring sessions |
| **Shop** | Order helmets, boots, breeches, kits, etc. |
| **Orders** | Track shop orders, payment & delivery status |
| **Payments** | See recorded payments and open the payment form |

At the top you also see quick counts: **Class Units**, **No-Shows**, **Upcoming**, **Participants**, **Payments**.

## 2. Sessions

**Purpose:** See what is booked and what already happened.

### 2.1 What each badge means

| Badge | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Scheduled** / **Upcoming** | Class is booked and still ahead |
| **Attended** | Marked Present by the trainer |
| **No-Show** | Rider did not attend |
| **Rescheduled** | Date/time was moved |

Each session card can show: service, date, time, trainer, participants, and class / curriculum title (with a document link when available).

### 2.2 Reschedule a class

1. Open **Sessions**.
2. Find an **upcoming** session (not already attended / completed / cancelled).
3. Tap **Reschedule**.
4. Choose a **new date**, optional **new time**, and optional **reason**.
5. Tap **Confirm Reschedule**.

You should see a success message; the list refreshes with the new date/time.

## 3. Curriculum

**Purpose:** Follow the rider’s training journey.

- Expand levels / classes to see what is completed.
- Open class documents when a link is shown.
- Performance chart (when available) summarises progress over time.

This tab is **read-only** — trainers update curriculum scores from the Stable Management app.

## 4. Book sessions

**Purpose:** Request new classes.

### 4.1 Single / Multi (specific dates)

1. Open **Book**.
2. Leave mode on **Single / Multi**.
3. Choose **Service**, **Date**, **Time**.
4. For one-time services, set **No. of Participants** (include yourself).
5. Tap **+ Add Another Session** if you need more dates.
6. Tap **Submit Sessions**.

### 4.2 Recurring (weekly pattern)

1. Switch to **Recurring**.
2. Pick the **month**, **days of week**, **pattern**, service, and time.
3. Tap **Submit Sessions**.

**Suggested next class** may appear at the top when curriculum has a clear next step — useful before you book.

---

## 5. Shop (Riding Essentials)

**Purpose:** Order kit and equipment from the campus catalog.

### 5.1 Place an order

1. Open **Shop**.
2. For each product: choose **size** (or type size / UK shoe size when asked) and **quantity**.
3. Tap **Add to cart**.
4. Review the cart → tap **Place Order**.
5. Note your **Order ID** (example: `ORD-20260812-0001`).

### 5.2 After you place an order

- If money is due, you will see the **shopping balance**, UPI / QR (when shown), and a **Complete payment form** button.
- On the payment form:
  - **Registration No** = your **KE Number**
  - **Payment For** = **Shopping Kit / Equipment**
- Shopping payments are **shared across all your shop orders** (oldest first). Extra credit stays on your shopping balance.

**Sizes:** If you are unsure, note “unknown” / coordinate in the WhatsApp group as guided by the school. Products without a price show **Price being updated** and cannot be ordered yet.

## 6. Orders — status guide (important)

Open **Orders** any time to see every shop order for this rider.

At the top you may see a summary:

| Line | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Total ordered** | Sum of all your shop order totals |
| **Shopping payments** | Money already recorded for shopping |
| **Remaining** | Still to pay |
| **Extra paid** | Credit left after covering orders |

Each order card shows: **Order ID**, date, products + sizes, total, payment line, and **order status**.

### 6.1 Order status (what happens to the order)

This is the main progress of your kit order:

| Status | What it means | What you should do |
|--------|---------------|--------------------|
| **Order Placed** | Order is recorded. Payment may still be pending. Team has not started packing/processing yet. | Complete the **shopping payment** if balance remains. |
| **In Progress** | Payment is in order (paid / verified) and the team is preparing your items. Expected delivery may be set. | Wait for delivery / pickup as advised by the campus. Check **Orders** for notes. |
| **Partially Delivered** | Some items in the order are marked delivered; others are still waiting. | Collect remaining items when the school notifies you. |
| **Delivered** | All items in this order are marked delivered by the team. | Confirm you received everything if the school asks. Contact campus if something is missing. |

**Simple flow:**

`Order Placed` → (pay) → `In Progress` → `Delivered`  
*(sometimes via `Partially Delivered` if items arrive in stages)*

### 6.2 Payment status (money for that order)

Shown on the same card as **Payment: … · Paid ₹… · Balance ₹…**

| Payment status | What it means | What you should do |
|----------------|---------------|--------------------|
| **Pending** | No shopping payment (or not enough credit) applied to this order yet. | Use **Make shopping payment** — enter KE No and choose Shopping Kit / Equipment. |
| **Partially Paid** | Some money applied; balance still remains. | Pay the remaining balance the same way. |
| **Paid** | This order’s total is fully covered by shopping payments / credit. | Nothing for payment. Order can move to **In Progress** / delivery. |
| **Submitted** | Payment form was submitted; team may still be verifying. | Wait for confirmation; contact campus if it stays here a long time. |
| **Submitted - Amount Mismatch** | Form amount did not match the expected order total. | Contact campus / re-submit the correct amount. |
| **Verified** | Staff confirmed payment (admin path). | Same as paid — team can process the order. |

**Note:** Shopping money is pooled. Paying once can cover several orders. The **Orders** tab recalculates Paid / Balance for each order from oldest to newest.

### 6.3 Product lines on the order

Under each order you see every product line:

- **Awaiting delivery** — not handed over yet  
- **✓ Delivered** (with date) — that item was marked delivered  

### 6.4 Quick FAQ for orders

**Why is my order still “Order Placed”?**  
Usually payment is still pending, or the team has not moved it forward yet. Check **Payment** on the card.

**I paid but status did not change.**  
Confirm the payment form used your **KE Number** and **Shopping Kit / Equipment**. Allow a short time for the ledger to update, then re-open **Orders**. If it still looks wrong, message the campus with Order ID + transaction reference.

**Can I cancel or change an order in the portal?**  
No — contact the campus / WhatsApp group. Staff update status from Stable Management.

**Where do I see school fees vs shopping?**  
**Payments** lists all recorded transactions (including type). **Orders** is only for riding essentials / kit.

## 7. Payments

**Purpose:** History of money recorded against this rider, and submit a new payment **inside My Rides** (no Google Form).

### 7.1 View past payments
1. Open **Payments**.
2. Each card shows amount, date, payment type, receipt number, and transaction reference when available.

### 7.2 Make a payment (in-app form)
1. Tap **+ Make a Payment** (or **Make shopping payment** from **Orders** / after checkout).
2. Fill the form:
   - **Registration No** and **Phone** — auto-filled  
   - **Amount**  
   - **Payment date**  
   - **Screenshot** of the transfer (required)  
   - **Transaction reference** — optional  
   - **PAN / Aadhaar** — auto-filled if you paid before  
   - **Mode** — UPI / Bank transfer / Cheque  
   - **Payment for** — Riding Classes, or Shopping Kit / Equipment (locked when paying for orders)
3. Optional: scan the UPI QR shown for the amount, then tap **Submit payment**.
4. Receipt is emailed after the payment is recorded.

Shopping payments are shared across all shop orders (oldest first).

## 8. Tips & troubleshooting

| Issue | What to try |
|-------|-------------|
| “Not found” on login | Use the exact registered phone or full KE Number for this campus. |
| Wrong child’s data | Sign out → login with phone → pick the correct profile. |
| Old order / session info | Sign out and sign in again, or hard-refresh the page. |
| Shop image missing | Product still orderable; images depend on campus Drive setup. |
| Cannot add to cart | Product may be unpriced (“Price being updated”) or size missing. |
| Need help | Call / WhatsApp the campus number shown in the portal footer. |

---

## 9. Privacy & sharing

- My Rides uses **phone or KE Number** only — treat the link like a family portal, not a public website.
- Prefer **Sign out** on shared devices.
- Use the **campus-specific** link you were given (Hyderabad / Pune / Bangalore URLs are different).
